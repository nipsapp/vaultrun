Circuit Breach

Game Rules

Crack the vault in three moves: tumble wins, collect chips with keys, then land Circuits to breach.

  1. 1
    Spin the 5×4 grid

    Pays are left-to-right ways — matching symbols on adjacent reels from the left. Up to 1,024 ways.

  2. 2
    Wins tumble

    Winning symbols explode. New symbols fall in. Every cascade win steps up the Breach Mult:

    1×2×3×5×8×12×

    Breach Mult resets on the next paid spin.

  3. 3
    Keys collect Cash Chips

    When tumbles stop, each Key collects every visible Cash Chip:

    Collect = bet × (sum of chip faces) × (number of keys)

    You need at least one Key and one Chip. Two or more Keys can also nudge Breach Mult / upgrade the Vault Gauge in free spins.

  4. 4
    3+ Circuits → Vault Breach

    Land three or more Circuit scatters anywhere to start free spins. Scatter count sets spin count and starting Vault Gauge.

Special symbols do the heavy lifting. Pay symbols are listed under Paytable.

Drill Wild

Substitutes for all pay symbols (not Key, Chip, or Circuit). Each Drill has its own mult: 2× / 3× / 5× / 10×. Wild mults on a win multiply together (capped at 50×) before Breach Mult.

Key

Does not pay ways. After cascades, every Key collects all Cash Chip values on the board.

Cash Chip

Face values × bet: 0.5 · 1 · 2 · 5 · 10 · 25 · 50 · 100. Collected only when Keys are present.

Circuit Scatter
