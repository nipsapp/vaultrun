How to Play

Ways

Base game is 5 wide × 4 high ways (left to right). Drill Wilds substitute for pay symbols and carry a personal multiplier.

Tumble & Breach Mult

Winning symbols explode and new ones tumble in. Each cascade advances the Breach Mult (1× → 2× → 3× → 5× → 8× → 12×).

Keys & Cash Chips

After cascades settle, every Key collects the sum of all visible Cash Chip values (× bet × number of keys).

Vault Breach

Land 3+ Circuit scatters to enter free spins. One feature — scatter count only changes spin count and starting Vault Gauge (Safe Crack → Strongroom → Vault Floor). Land 2+ Keys in a cascade to upgrade the Gauge.
