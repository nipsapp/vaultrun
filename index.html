How to Play

Ways

Base game is 6 wide × 3 high ways (left to right). Wild substitutes for pay symbols.

Lock & Respin

When the same premium symbol lands on reels 1, 2 and 3, that symbol and all wilds lock. Everything else respins. Locked symbols drop to the bottom. If another locked symbol or wild lands, it respins again until none land.

Barrel Wilds

If wilds are on the board and a pay symbol covers all reels left to right, wilds turn gold. After respins finish, barrels open and reveal a global multiplier: 2×, 3×, 5×, 10×, 15× or 20× applied to all wins.

Bonus Board

Free spins expand the board (up to 6×7). Extra rows start blocked with ↔ / ↕ arrows. When a locked symbol or wild lands next to a block, it breaks — arrows clear the row or column (Space Miners–style).
